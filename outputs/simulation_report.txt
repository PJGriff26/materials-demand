  - Calculates annual capacity additions
  - Models retirements based on technology-specific lifetimes

DATA QUALITY & LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

Material Intensity Data:
  - Source: compiled material-intensity literature
  - Uncertainty captured via fitted (lognormal) distributions
  - Small samples (n<5) use CV borrowing, NOT bootstrap; the
    fit is always parametric (see src/cv_borrowing.py)

Capacity Projections:
  - Source: NREL Standard Scenarios 2024
  - Reported at 3-year intervals (2026, 2029, 2032, ...)
  - Interpolated to an annual grid; default is shape-preserving
    PCHIP (see src/interpolation.py and --interpolation)

Model Limitations:
  - Technology mapping based on expert judgment
  - Some technologies lack intensity data (batteries, DAC, etc.)
  - No material recovery/recycling modeled
  - Assumes constant material intensity over time
